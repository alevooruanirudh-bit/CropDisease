    Tips for a good photo
    • Use daylight, not flash, and keep the camera steady.
    • Fill most of the photo with the affected leaf, fruit, stem or tuber.
    • Show the symptoms clearly: spots, mould, holes or discoloured areas.
    • Photograph one plant part at a time. A plain background helps.
    Trained on public plant-disease image datasets (PlantVillage, PlantDoc, AgML collections and more). Advice is general guidance; check with your local agricultural extension service before treating.
